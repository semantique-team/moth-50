# E-027 (E-021 arm 2) — TTS for acronyms, brands and code-switching

**Pre-registered 2026-11-23, before any TTS rendering, training or readout.**
Authorized by the owner on 2026-11-23 (option A after E-026). It is the one
training arm left under D34 (no new recordings) and has an explicit stop rule:
if it ties, the model line closes and E-008 plus the model card follow.

> **Result (2026-11-24): gate 1 tied and gate 4 failed; not adopted, the model
> line is closed (D36).** `runs/E-027/RESULT.md`, including a report-only look
> (2026-11-25) at why `switch` got worse: the `it` mode below also read
> `control_en` rows and long English runs with the Italian G2P.

## Question

Does a fine-tune of the product acoustic model (R1 seed 0) with permissively
licensed synthetic speech of mixed IT–EN sentences improve R-S1-cal WER or ETA
over a same-length fine-tune without it, without regressing the product on
S1-dev and dev-macro? The target errors are acronyms, brands and `switch`
(the residual risk carried from E-023, D34); E-026 showed that the product's
remaining term errors concentrate there.

## Text (fixed)

- **Source:** the 3,522 E-025 sentences (`runs/E-025/filtered.jsonl`,
  Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, Apache-2.0, seeded only with training-side terms;
  already filtered against S1-dev, T and A word 4-grams).
- **Re-filter for TTS** (`tools/e027_tts_script.py`): drop sentences with
  digits (the normalizer and the voices would read them differently), keep
  4–30 normalized words, drop every sentence sharing a word 4-gram with
  **S1-dev, T, A or R-S1-cal**, drop duplicates. Output
  `runs/E-027/tts-script.jsonl` and its report.
- **Language tags** for the mixed rendering: a word is English if it is a
  listed term (`en_terms.txt`) or an English function word, or if it occurs in
  the English training text (gold-train English spans, LibriSpeech-100) and is
  not an Italian training word seen at least 3 times (MLS-IT, CV-IT).
  `control_en` rows are English throughout, `control_it` rows Italian
  throughout; an elided prefix (`l'`, `dell'`...) is split off before an
  English word.

## Voices (fixed; files pinned by sha256 in `data/sources.lock.json`)

| Key | Engine | Voice | Modes | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sara | Kokoro-82M v1.0 (`f3ff3571…`) | `if_sara` (F) | it, mix | Apache-2.0 |
| nicola | Kokoro-82M v1.0 | `im_nicola` (M) | it, mix | Apache-2.0 |
| paola | Piper (`piper-voices` `e9c8a394…`) | `it_IT-paola-medium` (F) | it | MIT; data CC0, base Hi-Fi TTS 92 CC-BY-4.0 |
| serena | Piper | `it_IT-serena-medium` (F) | it | MIT; data CC-BY-4.0 |

- **Mode `it`:** the whole sentence through the Italian G2P (espeak-ng `it`);
  English terms come out Italian-accented, as many Italian speakers say them.
- **Mode `mix`** (Kokoro only, rows with at least one English word): English
  runs through espeak-ng `en-us`, Italian runs through `it`, joined phonemes
  spoken by the same Italian voice.
- **Excluded before rendering:** Piper `riccardo` (x_low, 16 kHz, M-AILABS
  licence not verified); Qwen3-TTS (no native Italian preset voice, 1.7 B
  generation cost on the 16 GB M5, needs an ASR hallucination filter); any
  voice clone of S1, S2, S3 or another real person.
- **Provenance risk, recorded:** Kokoro v1.0's card says part of its training
  audio is synthetic audio from closed TTS providers; serena was trained on
  Qwen3-TTS synthetic audio over a corpus with ~10 % LLM-written sentences.
  E-027 trains on the open voices' outputs, not on any API output, and the
  README data decision already admits Kokoro; the owner may veto a voice
  before rendering.

## Rendering, QC and audit (fixed; no evaluation data involved)

1. `tools/e027_tts_render.py` verifies every pinned file, renders each row in
   each applicable voice/mode, peak-normalizes to 0.7 and keeps a clip only if
   it lasts 0.8–30 s at 0.8–6.0 words/s (Kokoro phoneme strings over 500
   symbols are refused, not truncated). Output: WAVs under
   `data/raw/tts_e027/`, manifest `data/manifests/tts_e027.jsonl`
   (`source: tts_e027`, `split: train`, `speaker: tts-<voice>`, per-clip
   sha256), report `runs/E-027/render-report.json` with package versions. The
   installed engine versions are the ones of the single rendering; there is no
   re-render.
2. **Smoke first:** `--limit 5` for every voice and mode; a listener checks the
   ten-odd files before the full rendering. Fixing engine-API problems in the
   tool is allowed at this point: nothing has been measured.
3. **Listening audit** (`tools/e027_audit.py`): 10 clips per voice/mode stratum
   (6 strata), seeded; each is `ok` or `fail` (unintelligible, wrong or missing
   words, truncated, noise; an Italian accent is not a failure). A stratum with
   **2 or more fails is dropped** and the manifest rewritten without it, before
   the mel shards and the share.
4. **Share:** `tools/e027_share.py` sets the TTS `repeat` so the synthetic
   audio is about **10 %** of an epoch:
   `repeat = max(1, round(0.1 / 0.9 × base_hours / tts_hours))`; the value is
   written into `training/configs/base6-e027-tts.toml` and committed with the
   script, manifest and reports **before the first training step**.

## Training (fixed)

| | Control | TTS arm |
|---|---|---|
| Config | `training/configs/base6-e027-control.toml` | `training/configs/base6-e027-tts.toml` |
| Start | R1 seed 0 `ckpt_best.pt` (step 40,000, EMA weights, sha256 `5ff95f0e…`) | same |
| Data | the R1 mixture, unchanged | the same + `tts_e027` at the computed repeat |
| Optimizer | AdamW, `lr_head` 1e-4, `lr_encoder` 1e-5, warm-up 500, cosine to 5 % | same |
| Length | 8,000 steps (~0.6 epoch), EMA 0.999, bf16, checkpointing | same |
| Seeds | 0 and 1 | 0 and 1 |

The only difference between the arms is the TTS source
(`tests/test_training_config.py` asserts it). The trainer's new `[finetune]`
table loads the weights strictly and refuses a checkpoint with another sha256;
optimizer, schedule, step and EMA restart. The readout model is
**`ckpt_last.pt` at step 8,000, EMA weights**; the greedy dev-macro
`ckpt_best.pt` is monitoring only. Cost: about 3.2 h per run on the M5,
~13 h for the four.

## Readouts and gate (fixed before any readout)

All readouts: INT8 export with the E-034c LM, frozen decoder 0.55 / 2.7, beam
12, top-k 10, `tools/frozen_readout.py`. Seed means and paired bootstrap 1,000
(seed 0) with `tools/seed_mean.py` (a repeated `--control` is its own seed
mean). **A** = TTS arm, **C** = control, **P** = the product R1 s0
(`runs/E-023/*-s0.hyp.jsonl`). Differences are A − reference.

1. **Calibration win, A vs C on R-S1-cal:** WER CI entirely below zero and ETA
   CI not entirely below zero, **or** ETA CI entirely above zero and WER CI not
   entirely above zero.
2. **No loss against the product, A vs P on R-S1-cal:** WER CI not entirely
   above zero and ETA CI not entirely below zero.
3. **S1-dev veto, A vs P** (E-002 §6.2): WER CI not entirely above zero, ETA
   CI not entirely below zero.
4. **dev-macro guard, A vs P:** macro WER CI not entirely above zero.
5. **INT8 cost** on dev-macro (A INT8 − A FP32, seed mean) at most 0.2 pp.

**Report first, never gating** (E-002 §8.4): `acronym`, `brand`, `switch` and
`control_en` ETA/WER on R-S1-cal and S1-dev; each seed; C vs P (the effect of
fine-tuning alone); the render, audit and share reports. R-S1-cal resolves
about 1 pp WER; an arm below that ends as a tie.

**Outcome.**

- **Gate 1 fails → not adopted, and the model line closes:** no further
  training arm is planned (D34); next are E-008 (CS-FLEURS-READ once, as the
  external number) and the model card.
- **Gate 1 passes, any of 2–5 fails → not adopted;** the owner decides, no
  automatic follow-up arm.
- **All pass →** the seed is chosen by the E-002 §8.3 rule (R-S1-cal pooled
  ETA), then a **T-600 release guard**, a declared repeated read of that seed
  only, against `runs/E-023/t600-s0.hyp.jsonl`: pooled WER CI not entirely
  above zero and ETA CI not entirely below zero; S1/S2/S3 (S3 first: Moth D42)
  and `switch` / `control_en` report-only. Pass → package `0.9.8-e027-tts`
  (the release NOTICE gains Kokoro-82M, the Piper voices and their data
  attributions), browser check, FLEURS once (report-only, against
  `runs/E-023/fleurs-*-s0`), and a dated decision. Fail → R1 seed 0 stays.
