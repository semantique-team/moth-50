# Architecture review — 2026-11-17

**Status: review of the accepted D8 proposal; no training.** The proposal
(Whisper-base + 6 extra blocks + CTC + E-031 LM + Silero VAD, ~48 MB) is
**confirmed**. This review checked every Moth number cited in
`docs/architecture-options.md` against the Moth records, measured the real INT8
size, CPU latency and activation memory of the candidates, and lists what must
change before E-003. Corrections are applied in place in
`docs/architecture-options.md`; proposals are marked **proposed** and are not
decisions until `docs/decisions.md` says so.

Sources: the Moth development checkout `moth-stt-dev` at `d5ae605`
(`EXPERIMENTS.md`, `STATUS.md`, `docs/02`, `docs/10`–`docs/14`,
`docs/12-experiment-ledger.md`, `runs/E-0xx/`, `baselines/`); measurements in
`runs/probe-2026-11-17/`. Line numbers below refer to that checkout; the frozen
repository at `f3ecd1b` may differ slightly.

## 1. Verdict

- **Architecture: confirmed.** Whisper-base + CTC is the right family at 50 MB,
  and base+6 is the deepest dense model that fits with the A3 LM and Silero.
- **Package arithmetic: confirmed and conservative.** The measured graph is
  39.95 MB (not 40.4), the package 47.74 MB (not 48.2), the headroom 2.26 MB.
- **Training feasibility: the memory estimate was optimistic.** Without
  activation checkpointing, base+6 is projected at 12–21 GB on a 16 GB machine.
- **Evidence: nine cited numbers were wrong or misattributed and four had no
  source.** One of them (dev 30.86) was the baseline of the E-003 gate.
- **Code: the Moth model is shape-generic, the pipeline is not.** Six call sites
  build the tiny encoder by default; one silently records the wrong encoder in
  the training recipe.

- INT8 costs 1.0175 B/param at width 512 (1.026 at 384); the head and conv stem

  matrices (materialised `T × T` scores + an fp32 softmax copy). Per-block
  checkpointing brings it to ≈ 1 GB (estimate).

## 5. Proposals (status: proposed)

**P1 — Memory plan for the first base run.** Per-block activation checkpointing
from the start (≈ 1 GB of activations for base+6 instead of 9.7 GB, one extra
forward pass per step); batch caps on Σ T² or a ≤ 20 s clip cap, not only on
audio-seconds; check whether `scaled_dot_product_attention` on MPS avoids the
fp32 copy. Gate: E-002 runs `tools/probe_activations.py --device mps` on the
6 × 30 s bucket with and without `--checkpointing` before any multi-hour run.

**P2 — Adaptation schedule from Whisper-base.** The §6.1 learning rates (encoder
5e-6–1e-5, head 1e-4) are E-028's gentle-fine-tune values for a checkpoint that
was already CTC-trained. Moth's adaptation from Whisper was E-004 (head on a
frozen encoder) → E-005 (everything, head/encoder LR 1e-3/1e-4, 1,500-step
warm-up). Proposal: (1) short head + extra-block warm-up on the frozen encoder,
(2) full fine-tune with the encoder LR screened in 3e-5–1e-4, (3) gentle gold
fine-tune at E-028's LRs. Screen the encoder LR inside E-003.

**P3 — Identity initialization of the extra blocks.** Zero the `out` and `mlp2`
weights and biases of each new block (block expansion, as in LLaMA Pro): every
block starts as the identity, step 0 is exactly Whisper-base, and the random
blocks do not disturb the pretrained encoder early in training. Moth's E-006
blocks used PyTorch's default random init. This also reduces the bias of a
4,000-step screen against the deeper arms. Cost: none. Decide in E-003.

**P4 — Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 as a pseudo-labelling teacher.** Already measured in
Moth's baselines: T-600 **10.98 % WER / 83.6 % ETA**, FLEURS-IT 3.65 %,
FLEURS-EN 8.17 %, 0 words on 240 non-music noise clips; CC-BY-4.0. It is the
strongest commercially usable teacher on this exact task, and data is the
expected bottleneck. Vocabulary and decoder differ, so use sequence-level
pseudo-labels with confidence filtering, not logit KD. Proposed as an E-006 arm.
The unlabeled audio must be commercially usable on its own; attribute the
teacher in the NOTICE.

**P5 — A tighter LM binary format.** `MOTHLMB1` stores 4 B per n-gram (uint16
token + int16 log-prob) although a token needs 11 bits. 11-bit tokens + 8-bit
quantized log-probs are ≈ 2.4 B per n-gram, ~40 % less. The unpruned E-034 LM
(12.31 MB; T-600 −0.77 significant in E-023) would then fit in ≈ 7.3 MB, inside
base+6's 7.64 MB of LM room — base+6 could have both the depth and the larger
LM. Requires a new magic (`MOTHLMB2`), Python and JS readers, parity tests and a
measured cost of the log-prob quantization on dev. Worth doing before E-004.

**P6 — Evaluation hygiene.** Re-measure the 0.8.1 dev reference under the
frozen protocol in E-001/E-002 (the E-003 gate referenced E-014). Measure seed
variance (2–3 seeds for the winning recipe; a second seed whenever the top two
E-003 arms are within ~1 pp). Put the held-out S1 mixed session into checkpoint
selection: in E-028 dev got worse while the product got better. Recalibrate the
decoder per arm on dev in E-004 (E-022 moved T-600 by 6.85 pp with decoder
settings alone). Run the browser WER comparison that E-041 never ran.

**P7 — Fallback before dropping to base+4.** If checkpointed base+6 is too slow,
run the extra blocks at 25 Hz (stride-2 pooling after the encoder): it halves
their compute and quarters their attention memory, with ~25 frames per second
still well above the 4–6 BPE tokens per second of speech.

**Kept as is:** Whisper-base, CTC, the 1,024-piece BPE, the 50,000,000-byte
decimal ceiling, Silero VAD (without it, 0.8.1 emits 258 words on 146 of 337
non-music noise clips) and the INT8 head (an fp32 head did not recover the rare
terms in E-030 and costs ~1.6 MB at width 512).
